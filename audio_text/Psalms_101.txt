1. [A Psalm of David]. I will sing of lovingkindness and justice: to you, O the LORD, will I sing praises.
2. I will behave myself wisely in a perfect way: Oh when will you come unto me? I will walk within my house with a perfect heart.
3. I will set no base thing before mine eyes: I hate the work of them that turn aside; It shall not cleave unto me.
4. A perverse heart shall depart from me: I will know no evil thing.
5. Whoso privily slandereth his neighbor, him will I destroy: Him that has a high look and a proud heart will I not suffer.
6. Mine eyes will be upon the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me: He that walks in a perfect way, he shall minister unto me.
7. He that worketh deceit shall not dwell within my house: He that speaketh falsehood shall not be established before mine eyes.
8. Morning by morning will I destroy all the wicked of the land; To cut off all the workers of iniquity from the city of the LORD.
