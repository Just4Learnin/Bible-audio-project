1. [A Song of Ascents]. Blessed is every one that feareth the LORD, That walks in his ways.
2. For you shall eat the labor of thy hands: Happy shalt thou be, and it will be well with thee.
3. Thy wife will be as a fruitful vine, In the innermost parts of thy house; Thy children like olive plants, Round about thy table.
4. Behold, thus shall the man be blessed That feareth the LORD.
5. the LORD bless thee out of Zion: And see thou the good of Jerusalem all the days of thy life.
6. Yea, see thou thy children's children. Peace be upon Israel.
