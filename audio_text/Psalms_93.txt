1. the LORD reigneth; He is clothed with majesty; the LORD is clothed with strength; he has girded himself therewith: The world also is established, that it cannot be moved.
2. Thy throne is established of old: you are from everlasting.
3. The floods have lifted up, O the LORD, The floods have lifted up their voice; The floods lift up their waves.
4. Above the voices of many waters, The mighty breakers of the sea, the LORD on high is mighty.
5. Thy testimonies are very sure: Holiness becometh thy house, O the LORD, for evermore.
