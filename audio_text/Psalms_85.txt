1. [For the Chief Musician. A Psalm for the sons of Korah]. the LORD, you have been favorable unto thy land; you have brought back the captivity of Jacob.
2. you have forgiven the iniquity of thy people; you have covered all their sin. {{Selah
3. you have taken away all thy wrath; you have turned [thyself] from the fierceness of thine anger.
4. Turn us, O God of our salvation, And cause thine indignation toward us to cease.
5. will you be angry with us for ever? will you draw out thine anger to all generations?
6. will you not quicken us again, That thy people may rejoice in thee?
7. Show us thy lovingkindness, O the LORD, And grant us thy salvation.
8. I will hear what God the LORD will speak; For he will speak peace unto his people, and to his saints: But let them not turn again to folly.
9. Surely his salvation is nigh them that fear him, That glory may dwell in our land.
10. Mercy and truth are met together; Righteousness and peace have kissed each other.
11. Truth springeth out of the earth; And righteousness has looked down from heaven.
12. Yea, the LORD will give that which is good; And our land shall yield its increase.
13. Righteousness shall go before him, And shall make his footsteps a way [to walk in] .
