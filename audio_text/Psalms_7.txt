1. [Shiggaion of David, which he sang unto the LORD, concerning the words of Cush a Benjamite]. O the LORD my God, in thee do I take refuge: Save me from all them that pursue me, and deliver me,
2. Lest they tear my soul like a lion, Rending it in pieces, while there is none to deliver.
3. O the LORD my God, if I have done this; If there be iniquity in my hands;
4. If I have rewarded evil to him that was at peace with me; (Yea, I have delivered him that without cause was mine adversary;)
5. Let the enemy pursue my soul, and overtake it; Yea, let him tread my life down to the earth, And lay my glory in the dust. {{Selah
6. Arise, O the LORD, in thine anger; Lift up thyself against the rage of mine adversaries, And awake for me; you have commanded judgment.
7. And let the congregation of the peoples compass thee about; And over them return thou on high.
8. the LORD ministereth judgment to the peoples: Judge me, O the LORD, according to my righteousness, and to mine integrity that is in me.
9. O let the wickedness of the wicked come to an end, but establish thou the righteous: For the righteous God trieth the minds and hearts.
10. My shield is with God, Who saveth the upright in heart.
11. God is a righteous judge, Yea, a God that has indignation every day.
12. If a man turn not, he will whet his sword; He has bent his bow, and made it ready.
13. He has also prepared for him the instruments of death; He makes his arrows fiery [shafts] .
14. Behold, he travaileth with iniquity; Yea, he has conceived mischief, and brought forth falsehood.
15. He has made a pit, and digged it, And is fallen into the ditch which he made.
16. His mischief shall return upon his own head, And his violence shall come down upon his own pate.
17. I will give thanks unto the LORD according to his righteousness, And will sing praise to the name of the LORD Most High.
