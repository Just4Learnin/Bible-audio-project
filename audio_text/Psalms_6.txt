1. [For the Chief Musician; on stringed instruments, set to the Sheminith. A Psalm of David]. O the LORD, rebuke me not in thine anger, Neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure.
2. Have mercy upon me, O the LORD; for I am withered away: O the LORD, heal me; for my bones are troubled.
3. My soul also is sore troubled: And thou, O the LORD, how long?
4. Return, O the LORD, deliver my soul: Save me for thy lovingkindness' sake.
5. For in death there is no remembrance of thee: In Sheol who shall give thee thanks?
6. I am weary with my groaning; Every night make I my bed to swim; I water my couch with my tears.
7. Mine eye wasteth away because of grief; It waxeth old because of all mine adversaries.
8. Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity; For the LORD has heard the voice of my weeping.
9. the LORD has heard my supplication; the LORD will receive my prayer.
10. All mine enemies will be put to shame and sore troubled: They shall turn back, they will be put to shame suddenly.
