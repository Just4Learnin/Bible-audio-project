1. [A Psalm of David, when he fled from Absalom his son]. the LORD, how are mine adversaries increased! Many are they that rise up against me.
2. Many there are that say of my soul, There is no help for him in God. {{Selah
3. But thou, O the LORD, art a shield about me; My glory and the lifter up of my head.
4. I cry unto the LORD with my voice, And he answereth me out of his holy hill. {{Selah
5. I laid me down and slept; I awaked; For the LORD sustaineth me.
6. I will not be afraid of ten thousands of the people That have set themselves against me round about.
7. Arise, O the LORD; Save me, O my God: For you have smitten all mine enemies upon the cheek bone; you have broken the teeth of the wicked.
8. Salvation belongeth unto the LORD: Thy blessing be upon thy people. {{Selah
